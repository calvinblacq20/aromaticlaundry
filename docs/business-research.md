## 3. Positioning: what makes it different

1. **Scent.** The name is the promise. The best review says: "I spent hours smelling them… washed so fast… called to pick up within hours."
2. **Mall convenience.** Customers drop laundry off while they shop. It is the only laundry listed in West Hills Mall, which is a large anchor mall serving the Weija / Kasoa / Mallam / Gbawe corridor.
3. **Speed and rain-proofing.** Machine drying is pitched against Accra's rainy season, when people can't line-dry.
4. **Special garments.** Suits, kente and stain rescue (palm-oil stain demo).
5. **Personal founder brand.** Friends and community regularly comment "patronize her", "Amodeeee", "Alla Dianaaa".

**Target customers:** busy professionals, mothers ("#momlife", "Mama"), families on the west side of Accra, and mall shoppers.

## 6. Gaps and risks

1. **No owned web presence.** There's no website, Google Maps/Business listing or Instagram. People searching "laundry near Weija" can't find the business.
2. **Booking is manual.** Every order goes through phone or WhatsApp. There's no order tracking, no record of customer history, and no reminders.
3. **The subscription is undefined.** The ₵400 plan has no item limit, pickup frequency or household rules. That causes confusion and puts margin at risk.
4. **Pricing is ambiguous.** "About 6–8 clothes" leads to disputes, the delivery fee is unknown, and the dry-cleaning price list is missing.
5. **Brand inconsistency.** There are three taglines, a "LAUNDARY" typo in one caption, and possibly two phone numbers.
6. **The express markup is steep for small loads.** +₵50 on a ₵50 basket doubles the price.
7. **Business depends on the founder.** Growth relies on Diane's personal network and her presence on TikTok.

## 7. Opportunities

- **Website and Google Business Profile.** Show the price list, plans, the mall map pin, hours and reviews, with a WhatsApp booking button. Target local SEO terms such as "laundry Weija", "laundry Kasoa", "dry cleaning West Hills Mall".
- **Clear subscription tiers.** For example: Solo ₵400 for about 50 items and 4 pickups; Family for more items. Take MoMo/Paystack recurring payments.
- **Simple ordering and tracking.** Book a pickup → status updates (picked up, washing, ready, out for delivery) over WhatsApp or SMS.
- **Content engine.** A weekly "stain of the week" before/after, seasonal hooks (rain, Christmas, school reopening, funerals and weddings for kente), customer testimonials, and price-led posts.
- **Mall partnerships.** Shop-and-drop with mall anchors, and corporate packages for offices along the corridor.
- **Loyalty and referral.** Offers like "10th basket free" or "refer a friend, get ₵20 off" build repeat business.
